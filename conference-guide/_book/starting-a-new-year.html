5  Starting a new conference year

Once a year, after the conference wraps up, the site moves to the next year. The checklist of record is conferences/_template/README.md. This chapter is the overview so you know what the steps are for.

5.1 The steps

  1. Archive the year that ended. Move the live files (conferences/index.qmd, index.css, images/, data/, cfp/, and preconference/) into conferences/archive/ under the year that just ended. Then open the archived index.qmd and change its two archive links (the Archives tile and the Learn More button) to ../index.qmd so they lead back to the new current page. The archived pages keep reading their own year’s data/ folder, so they stay frozen in time.
  2. Copy the template. Copy everything in conferences/_template/ up into conferences/. Copy, do not move, so the template stays clean for the following year.
  3. Replace the placeholders. The comment at the top of the new index.qmd lists every placeholder: the year, city, dates, and hotel. “Replace in Files” in RStudio or VS Code handles each one in a single pass. The same placeholders appear in the seed CSVs in data/, so run the replacements there too.
  4. Add the images. Save the new conference logo as images/logo.png and a hotel photo as images/hotel.png in conferences/images/.
  5. Set up the Google Sheet. Hand the conference Sheet to the new co-chairs (or make next year’s copy with the structure documented in conferences/_update-conference.R), then run source("conferences/_update-conference.R") to pull it into the site.

5.2 One step outside the conference folder

The Conferences menu in the site navbar names the current year. After the rollover, open _quarto.yml in the project root, find the Conferences menu, and update the label to the new year. The link itself does not change; only the label does.

5.3 Then render and hand off

Render the conference pages and check them in the browser: the main page, cfp/index.qmd, and preconference/index.qmd. When they look right, share the changed files with the web editor as described in UPDATING.md.

The new page works immediately after step 3, with “coming soon” notes everywhere. The rest of the year runs through the Google Sheet, as the previous chapter describes.
